Understanding lead-wire compensation

2-wire

Both cable conductors are in series with the sensor, so their combined resistance adds to the measured value. The optional software correction subtracts an entered total lead resistance; it is an estimate, not a change to the electrical measurement.

3-wire

A proper 3-wire instrument circuit compensates lead resistance when the lead resistances are approximately equal. Selecting 3-wire here only explains that method; this page does not simulate that circuit or apply a manual correction.

4-wire

A proper 4-wire (Kelvin) measurement separates excitation and sensing leads, effectively eliminating lead resistance from the resistance measurement. No manual lead correction is applied here.
