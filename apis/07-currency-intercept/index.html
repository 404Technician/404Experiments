Source notes / two separate clocks

Frankfurter combines daily exchange-rate data from central banks and official sources. Conversion and window changes are calculated here; these are observations for comparison.

CBS dataset 80416ned publishes nationally weighted daily fuel averages in weekly batches, normally on Thursday for observations through Monday. Figures are definitive, available from 1 January 2006, and supplied by Travelcard BV. Observation dates differ from dataset update dates. No station-specific prices or causal relationship between these two signals is implied.
